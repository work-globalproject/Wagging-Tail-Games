A little help for playtime.

Wagging Tail Games · Version 1.0.0 · Leo Boy Games

Getting started

Make a dog profile, choose a game and follow its steps. Use the timer to record a session, then view your history in Stats. All V1 games are included; sharing and accounts are optional.

Play comfortably

Supervise every game, use dog-safe toys that cannot be swallowed, and choose a clear, non-slip space. Keep sessions comfortable and stop if your dog wants to rest. Ask your veterinarian about suitable activity for puppies or dogs with health or mobility concerns. The app's times and goals are suggestions, not exercise prescriptions.

Cloud backup and changing phones

Sign in with the same email on another device. Account has a Sync option and reports unsuccessful uploads. Guest history is stored separately and is copied into your account only when you choose Import this device's guest history. Your current cloud dog profile is preserved during import if it is already set up.

Forgotten password

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen. Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset link. Never send your password to support.

Camera and saved moments

Grant camera permission in device Settings, or select a photo. Videos are silent. Choose Save / Share before closing the preview to keep the file. Activity history stores session details, not photos or videos.

Daily reminders

Enable a reminder in the app and allow notifications. The time comes from your dog profile. You can disable reminders in the app or device Settings. Focus modes and operating-system scheduling may delay notifications.

Deleting an account

Open Account, choose Delete account and cloud data, enter your password and confirm. Keep the app online until it finishes. If it fails, retry; some records may already be deleted. Use Forgot password first if you need an email password for an older social-login account.

Contact

Contact the creator through @leoboylondon on Instagram. Include your app version, device model, and what happened. Do not include passwords or private videos. Read the privacy notice.
