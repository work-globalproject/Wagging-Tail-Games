Your play. Your privacy.

Privacy notice for Wagging Tail Games by Leo Boy Games. Updated September 8, 2026.

Playing as a guest

Your dog's profile, preferences and play history are saved on your device. You can play without creating an account. The app fetches its shared game catalog from Firebase; this network connection necessarily provides connection information such as your IP address to the service.

Optional cloud backup

If you create an account, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email, password and optional display name to sign you in. Firebase Firestore stores your account identifier, profile, dog profile, session details, notes and activity totals. We use these to provide backup, restore history and administer the app. Authorized app administrators can access account and session records to operate and support the service. Firebase processes service data under its own terms and privacy documentation. Read about Firebase privacy and security.

Photos, videos and sharing

Camera access is optional. V1 records silent video and does not request microphone access. Captures and selected photos are temporary device previews; they are not uploaded to our cloud database. Export before closing the camera screen to keep a copy. When you use Save / Share, your device lets you choose a destination. That destination's privacy practices apply. Temporary share files are removed after the share sheet returns; copies you export remain under your control.

Notifications, analytics and advertising

Daily reminders are optional local notifications. You can turn them off in the app or device settings. This V1 does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking or Firebase Analytics. Signed-in account activity totals and last-active times are stored to operate the service; they are visible to authorized administrators.

Retention and deletion

Cloud account data remains until you delete the account. In Account, choose Delete account and cloud data and confirm your password. This removes your Firebase sign-in account, cloud profile and sessions and this account's local app data on the current device. Failed deletion can be retried. Guest history, exported files and copies cached on other devices are separate; clear guest data in the app or remove the app/browser data on those devices. Signing out alone does not erase your device cache. Service providers may retain operational logs or backups according to their retention policies.

Your choices and contact

Use the app without an account, import guest history only when you choose, deny camera access, disable reminders, or delete your account. For questions or data requests, visit our support page. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete personal data and to contact your local privacy authority.

The app is intended for adult dog owners. It is a play-ideas tool, not veterinary care or a service aimed at children.
